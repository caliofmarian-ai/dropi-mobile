# Legal Gaps and Launch Blockers

**Version:** 1.8.0
**As of:** 2026-09-16
**Default:** every item below blocks only its affected scope, but no affected scope may be enabled by an override.

This register prevents an unanswered legal question from becoming an accidental product assumption. Resolution requires a primary/current source, written analysis, scoped approval, updated traceability, and implementation/test evidence.

## Explicit non-assumptions

- No source captured so far establishes a universal government attestation for a parcel courier riding an ordinary pedal bicycle in either country.
- A bicycle safety course, medical policy, helmet rule, or personal-accident cover may be imposed by DROPi, contract, employer, LGU, or insurer; its source must be labeled accurately.
- A PFA, sole proprietor, corporate registration, PSIC/CAEN code, driving licence, vehicle registration, or uploaded document is not by itself service authorization.
- Passenger authority does not authorize parcel/postal service; parcel authority does not authorize passengers.
- A drone controller certificate does not authorize the company, aircraft, route, payload operation, airspace, or delivery CONOPS by itself.
- A foreign company registration does not automatically authorize local platform, postal, transport, privacy, tax, or aviation activity.
- DSA micro/small-enterprise exclusions do not mean `DSA does not apply`; the applicable obligation set must be derived from the actual service and enterprise status.
- P2B small-enterprise exceptions are obligation-specific and do not mean `P2B does not apply`; the P2B size test must not be reused for accessibility.
- A professional/company merchant is not automatically an excluded seller for DAC7; platform-operator, activity and seller status require the current factual/statutory analysis.
- The DAC7 sale-of-goods `<30 / <= EUR 2,000` de-minimis rule is conjunctive and does not replace other excluded-seller checks.
- A new, low-revenue or expected-small company is not automatically exempt from Romanian e-commerce accessibility duties; the statutory microenterprise evidence must be tested under the accessibility definition, not Romanian fiscal microenterprise status.
- Marketplace-provider or seller-of-record status does not by itself identify the producer/EPR-responsible actor for product, grouped, transport or shipping packaging.
- Merchant-managed fulfilment does not automatically remove a PPWR online-platform producer-verification duty where the PPWR/DSA factual scope is met.
- A PPWR online-platform verification duty does not automatically make DROPi the packaging producer/EPR-responsible actor.
- A CAEN code describes/classifies economic activity but does not replace sector authorization, notification or the authority's classification of the factual service.
- A PSP integration does not permit DROPi to describe itself as holding customer money, providing escrow or issuing stored value unless that exact regulated role is separately established.
- Provider split/payout functionality does not decide seller-of-record, supplier, VAT, invoice or RO e-Factura responsibilities.
- Official authority guidance may inform design/review but does not become binding law merely because it is useful; its authority class must remain explicit.
- A future-effective provision visible in a consolidated legal source is not active before its legal effective date.

## Closure protocol

For every gap:

1. archive/register the current primary source;
2. cite the exact provision, version, authority and effective date;
3. obtain qualified interpretation and written confirmation where required;
4. separate legal duties from DROPi/insurer/provider policy;
5. update the requirement and company/partner/product gate;
6. implement and test the control;
7. record scoped approval and next review date;
8. enable only the approved entity/service/vehicle/zone stage.
