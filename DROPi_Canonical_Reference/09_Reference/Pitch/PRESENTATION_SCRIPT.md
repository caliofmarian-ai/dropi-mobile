### Visual
- Revenue streams:

  - Emergency response: 15%

### Speaker Notes
"Our business model is diversified and resilient.

**Enterprise Contracts** (35% of revenue): Organizations pay monthly fees for dedicated logistics services. These are high-margin, recurring revenue streams.

**Premium Features** (10% of revenue): Customers pay for priority delivery, guaranteed time windows, and advanced tracking.

This diversification means we're not dependent on any single revenue stream. If marketplace demand drops, enterprise contracts sustain us. If enterprise demand drops, emergency response and premium features provide cushion."

## Presentation Tips

### Before the Presentation
1. Practice multiple times – aim for smooth delivery
2. Time yourself – stay within 30 minutes
3. Know your numbers – be ready for detailed questions
4. Prepare backup slides for common questions
5. Have printed materials ready

### During the Presentation
1. Start strong – capture attention in first 2 minutes
2. Tell stories – use customer examples and case studies
3. Show passion – let your enthusiasm shine through
4. Make eye contact – connect with your audience
5. Pause for questions – don't rush through slides
6. Use visuals – minimize text, maximize impact

### After the Presentation
1. Follow up promptly – send materials within 24 hours
2. Answer questions thoroughly – provide detailed responses
3. Schedule next steps – move conversations forward
4. Get feedback – learn what resonated and what didn't
5. Stay in touch – maintain relationships with prospects
